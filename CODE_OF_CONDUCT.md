# Code of Conduct

Wisp follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
It applies everywhere people work on Wisp together: issues, pull requests,
discussions and reviews.

In short: be kind, assume good intent, keep criticism about the work rather
than the person, and don't harass anyone. Maintainers may edit or remove
comments, close threads, or block people who don't follow it.

## Reporting a problem

If someone's behavior worries you, please tell the maintainer privately
rather than replying in public:

- Open a [private report](https://github.com/harlixay7/Wisp/security/advisories/new)
  through GitHub. The form is meant for security issues, but only the
  maintainers can see it, so it works for conduct reports too. Say in the
  title that it is about conduct.
- Or contact the maintainer, [@harlixay7](https://github.com/harlixay7),
  through GitHub.

Reports are kept confidential. The Contributor Covenant's
[enforcement guidelines](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/#enforcement-guidelines)
describe the steps a maintainer may take.
